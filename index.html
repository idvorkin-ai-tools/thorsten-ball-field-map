Thorsten Ball, mapped

Thorsten Ball is a software engineer and writer. He co-founded Amp, the coding agent, after years at Sourcegraph and Zed. He writes the weekly newsletter Register Spill, whose issues are now mostly called Joy & Curiosity, and co-hosts Raising an Agent with Quinn Slack. Before any of that he wrote two self-published books on building a programming language in Go. This site takes each body of work in turn.

The one-line version. Thorsten likes to open black boxes. His books show that an interpreter is plain, tested Go with no libraries. His best-known agent essay shows that a coding agent is an LLM, a loop and a few tools, in under 400 lines. The podcast is two people building one of those agents in public and reporting, every few weeks, on what it did to how they work. Start with who he is, or jump to How to Build an Agent.
